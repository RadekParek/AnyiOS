AnyiOSEMU runtime — third-party notices

AnyiOS keeps its existing app shell, shared game library and Converter. AnyiOSEMU is a separate Android-hosted ARM32 guest runtime integrated from HyperHLE-Reborn, a fork of touchHLE.
